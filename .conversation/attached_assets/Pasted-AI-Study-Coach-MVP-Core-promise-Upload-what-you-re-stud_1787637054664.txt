AI Study Coach — MVP

Core promise

“Upload what you’re studying. Your AI coach creates a personalized study plan, teaches you, tests you, and remembers what you struggle with.”

The MVP should revolve around one loop:

Learn → Practice → Get tested → Identify weaknesses → Review → Repeat

This is the main feature.

Instead of simply giving answers, the AI acts like a teacher.

Student:

Let’s break it down.

Student → Course → Professor

What problem might happen if one professor teaches multiple courses?
